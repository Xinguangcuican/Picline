# Picline icon concepts — v1

Created with the built-in image_gen tool using the imagegen skill. These are three independent candidates for review; the installed application icon has not been changed. No input/reference artwork was used.

## A — 悬挂卡片

File: Picline-A-hanging-cards.png

Prompt:

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a single production-quality macOS application icon concept for Picline, a small utility that hangs screenshots and copied images on a clothesline under the menu bar.
Concept A — Hanging Cards. Design a warm porcelain / off-white rounded-square macOS icon tile with two overlapping photo cards hanging from one thin gently sagging cord near the upper third. The cards are slightly staggered and subtly tilted, attached with tiny but clear teal clips. Their image areas use very simple aqua / sea-green / soft blue abstract landscape shapes, no detailed photos. Make the two-card silhouette immediately recognizable. Restrained sculptural depth, crisp smooth edges, soft ambient occlusion, tactile satin card stock, premium native Mac utility feel. Balanced centered composition with generous breathing room; the subject occupies roughly 72% of the tile. Clear readable shapes at small Dock sizes. Front view, no isometric camera. 1024 by 1024 square. The tile is rounded-square with transparent pixels outside its rounded corners, not a white page. No words, no letters, no app name, no watermark, no surrounding scene, no comparison board. Original artwork.
```

## B — 图片夹子

File: Picline-B-clip.png

Prompt:

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a single production-quality macOS application icon concept for Picline, a utility for collecting screenshots and images on a hanging line.
Concept B — The Clip. A friendly, bold, warm apricot / muted coral rounded-square tile. One off-white square photo card is held at its top by a distinctive oversized deep teal clothespin; a short cord passes behind the clothespin and suggests hanging. The clothespin is the main memorable silhouette, sculpted with just enough depth to feel tactile. The card has one very simple lavender and blue landscape shape, no detailed photograph. Slight playful tilt, broad calm color areas, soft rounded edges and restrained realistic shadows. Polished independent Mac app icon, inviting and clear at 32 pixels. Front-on centered composition, icon fills the tile with comfortable margins; no other objects. 1024 by 1024 square. Transparent pixels outside the rounded tile corners. No words, no app name, no typography, no badges, no watermark, no device mockup, no presentation board. Original artwork.
```

## C — 极简绳线

File: Picline-C-minimal-line.png

Prompt:

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a single production-quality macOS application icon concept for Picline, a small utility that hangs captured images from a line.
Concept C — Minimal Line. A rich midnight-navy rounded-square tile carrying one exceptionally simple custom mint / pale aqua line symbol. A gently suspended cord and a small empty rectangular image frame beneath it connect into one elegant continuous geometric mark, subtly suggesting a P through the relationship of vertical line and frame; this is a custom pictorial symbol, not a typeset letter. Use only a few confident broad strokes and one tiny warm amber attachment point. Flat graphic clarity with a very restrained soft bevel on the symbol, luxurious quiet contrast, precise negative space. No landscapes or photo details inside the empty frame. Memorable and extremely legible at small Dock sizes. Center the symbol and keep ample margin. Front-on, 1024 by 1024 square; transparent outside the rounded tile corners. No words, no app name, no typography, no watermark, no comparison board, no surrounding scene. Original artwork.
```
